[h1]New Nation: Portugal[/h1]

Portugal arrives in the Caribbean of 1660. This mod adds Portugal as a full playable nation. Cumaná and Caracas become Portuguese colonies, with a Governor-General, military ranks, a letter of marque and its own questline.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Portugal as a sixth nation, with its own diplomacy, flag, uniforms, ships and portraits.
[*]Cumaná and Caracas as Portuguese colonies, flying the Portuguese flag on the world map.
[*]Portugal in character creation: start your career as a Portuguese.
[*]"Flag of Portugal" ability to raise Portuguese colours on your ship.
[*]Portuguese letter of marque, from the governor or the diplomat.
[*]Portuguese ranks: Corsair, Captain, Capitão-Mor, Admiral, General, Governor.
[*]"For the Glory of the Crown" questline: 7 missions about a pirate network paid by Spain to ruin Portuguese trade in the Caribbean and Brazil. It has choices, informants who don't talk for free, and an ending where you find out who was using you.
[*]After the questline you become Governor-General. Each month you receive a tenth of the Portuguese colonies' profits into a treasury that accumulates, and you can move the seat of government.
[/list]

[h2]Important[/h2]
[list]
[*]Start a [b]new game[/b]. Old saves don't include Portugal.
[*]The questline is in English and Portuguese. Other languages show the English text.
[*]Music and voices still use the Spanish ones.
[*]The mod replaces 111 game files and may conflict with other mods that change the same files.
[*]Back up your game folder before installing.
[/list]
